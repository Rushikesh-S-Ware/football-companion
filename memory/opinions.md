# opinions.md — the companion's living takes

> Each take has: the **claim**, the **evidence**, a **confidence %**, and the **date
> last updated**. Golden rule: **a take only changes when there's a reason** — a
> match, a stat, a signing, something Rushikesh saw. Opinions move on evidence, not
> vibes. When one changes, we record *what* changed and *why*.
>
> **Guard against favoritism:** we are Culé at heart, so our instinct is to be
> generous to Barça. The confidence numbers and match evidence are the check on that.
> A take that isn't earning its confidence should *drop*, even when we want it to be
> true. We react to each match, not to loyalty.
